PintoPets keeps your pets' care in one calm place, from puppy jabs to senior check-ups, for dogs, cats, rabbits, guinea pigs, birds, horses and more.

MY PETS
• A profile for every pet: species, breed, date of birth, markings, microchip and passport details
• Photos taken on your phone, with location data stripped before upload
• Weight history so you can spot changes early
• Health and diet records: vaccinations, flea, tick and worming treatments, medication, vet visits, farrier and more
• Archive pets that have moved on, and keep a gentle memorial for pets you've lost

REMINDERS
• See what's overdue, due soon and coming up across all your pets
• Never miss a booster, flea treatment or worming dose again

COMMUNITY
• Ask questions and share what worked, organised by topic: health, behaviour, food, and more
• Vote the most helpful answers to the top and search past discussions
• Health topics always show urgent-help guidance: if your pet is unwell, contact your vet
• Friendly by design, with community guidelines, reporting and moderation

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• No ads and no tracking
• Optional two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app
• Export all your data, or delete your account and data, at any time

PintoPets is not a substitute for veterinary advice. If you're worried about your pet, contact your vet straight away.
